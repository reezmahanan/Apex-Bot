Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common help desk questions about Sri Lankan higher education rules.

Candidates must obtain a minimum of three 'S' passes in the Physical Science stream (Combined Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry or ICT) at a single sitting of the GCE Advanced Level examination, or equivalent foreign qualifications (Edexcel/Cambridge), matching UGC guidelines.

Mahapola scholarship stipends are disbursed as vouchers at the Student Welfare Division starting the 10th of every month. Students must physically collect their voucher by presenting their Student Record Book and National Identity Card (NIC). Vouchers can be cashed at the Bank of Ceylon (BOC) campus branch.

Connect to the 'Apex-Secure' WiFi network on your device. Log in using your student email account credentials (e.g., your Index Number followed by `@student.apex.edu.lk`) and your portal account password.

The Interest-Free Student Loan Scheme (IFSLS) is conducted by the Ministry of Education. Eligible students who didn't get state university entry can apply online through the official Ministry portal (www.moe.gov.lk) after GCE A/L university cuts are released.

The Main Campus Library is open on weekdays from 8:00 AM to 8:00 PM and on Saturdays from 8:30 AM to 5:00 PM. The library is closed on Sundays, public holidays, and Poya Days.
